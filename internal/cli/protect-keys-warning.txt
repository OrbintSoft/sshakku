From now on, any file created here will be encrypted, and your SSH server can't read encrypted files — it runs as the system, not as you. There's no authorized_keys here yet, so if one is ever added, SSH key logins to this machine will stop working and nothing will explain why.

Advice: keep your private keys where your SSH server never looks — "sshakku move-keys <directory>" does the whole move for you: the files, the permissions, and the setting that says where they went.
